## Image Specifications

**General requirements for all images:**
- High resolution (minimum 1920x1080px)
- Horizontal/landscape orientation preferred
- Good lighting and professional quality
- Authentic Indonesian/Javanese cultural context
- No obvious watermarks or text overlays
- Rights-cleared for commercial use

**File naming convention:**
- Use descriptive names: `event-slug-01.jpg`
- Example: `wayang-kulit-night-aug-2026-01.jpg`

**Storage location:**
- Save to: `/mock-backend/images/events/`
- Update `cover_image_url` in `db.json` after downloading
